**Every row here is checkable.** A claim with nothing to open is not on this page — that is the rule
the whole project is organised around, and it is the rule most easily broken on a competitive
document, where the temptation is to say the strong version.

Three things are true about the field and are said plainly rather than hedged:

- **These are good products.** MakerKit, Supastarter, BoxyHQ and ShipFast are well built, maintained,
  and worth their price to the people who buy them. Nothing below is a claim that their engineering
  is poor.
- **keelblock loses on breadth, deliberately.** Supastarter ships five payment providers, an AI
  chatbot and three frameworks; MakerKit ships TanStack Start and an Expo kit. Matching that is
  years of work and it is the bloat the field is criticised for. keelblock ships one good path per
  concern.
- **What is compared is architecture, not effort.** Where a rival's approach has a ceiling, the
  ceiling is what is named — not the team.

Sources for every competitor claim are in [`research/01-FIELD-SCAN.md`](../../research/01-FIELD-SCAN.md)
and the pinned corpus. Where a competitor's own repository is cited, the file and symbol are named so
it can be read rather than taken on trust.
